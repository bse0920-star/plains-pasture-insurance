How the Rainfall Index Actually Works

PRF doesn't insure your fence-line rain gauge. It protects against regional forage deficits and escalating supplemental feed costs.

1

We Map Your Grid ID

The RMA divides the map into roughly 17x17 mile grids. We map your owned or leased pasture tracts to identify your operation's exact Grid ID.

2

Backtest 20 Years of Data

We pull two decades of NOAA NCEI precipitation data to target two-month intervals matching your forage growth cycle (spring green-up vs. late summer dry spells).

3

Automatic Indemnities

If the published final grid index drops below your coverage level trigger (70%–90%), payments are direct deposited with no adjusters needed.
